HOW WE SHAPE THE PROJECT
01

Editorial

Footage review, assembly, narrative structure, pacing and picture edit.

02

Colour

Shot matching, correction and a grade that supports the film’s visual direction.

03

Sound & graphics

Dialogue cleanup, music and sound requirements, titles and motion graphics defined in the scope.

04

Final delivery

Review rounds, captions and agreed export specifications. Source files and project handover are agreed separately.

Start with the brief.

Tell us the audience, intended use, deadline and budget range. We’ll recommend a scope with deliverables, production needs and review milestones. Talent, locations, music licences, usage rights and additional versions are agreed in the proposal.
