Using this website

This website describes Decipher’s film and media services. An enquiry does not create a service contract or require a payment.

Project scope and fees

Film and media projects are quoted individually. The agreed proposal sets out the deliverables, fees, taxes, external costs, payment schedule, review rounds and timeline.

Production and changes

Timelines depend on the agreed scope and timely access, materials, feedback and approvals. Changes to the brief, shoot arrangements or deliverables may affect fees and dates and will be discussed before additional work proceeds.

Materials, rights and delivery

Clients should have permission to use the materials they supply. Talent usage, music and stock licences, ownership, source files and delivery formats are covered in the project agreement.

Cancellations and payments

Cancellation, rescheduling, refund and payment terms are agreed in writing for each project before it begins. No payments are collected through this website.

Third-party services

External video platforms and email services operate under their own terms. Their availability is outside Decipher’s direct control.

Questions

Contact Deciphermediahouse@gmail.com with questions about a proposed project or these terms.
