What shapes the quote?

Page count, content readiness, design complexity and functionality all affect scope. Domain registration, hosting, third-party subscriptions, custom add-ons and ongoing maintenance are quoted separately unless included in your proposal. Applicable taxes and payment milestones will be stated in the quote.

What is the difference between static and dynamic?

A static website presents information without a content-management backend. A dynamic website can include editable content, admin tools or data-driven features. We’ll recommend an approach after reviewing your brief.

Can you help with content and updates?

Yes. Copywriting, content entry, migration and maintenance can be discussed as part of a separate scope.

How long will my website take?

We agree a timeline after reviewing the pages, features and available content. Review turnaround and content readiness influence delivery.
