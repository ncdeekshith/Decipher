A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Before we begin.

What is a static website?＋

A static website presents content such as your business, services, and contact information. It suits businesses that need a clear online presence without custom databases or account workflows.

When should I choose a dynamic website?＋

Choose dynamic when you need changing data, content management, or business workflows. We’ll confirm the features and custom add-ons in your proposal.

Are hosting and domain costs included?＋

Hosting, domain, ongoing maintenance, taxes, and third-party costs are confirmed separately in your proposal. There are no automatic purchases through this website.

What do marketing and video services cost?＋

These services are quoted against the brief, production needs, campaign scope, and deliverables. Advertising spend and external production expenses are agreed separately.

How long does a project take?＋

The timeline depends on scope, content readiness, and review rounds. We agree on delivery milestones before work begins.
