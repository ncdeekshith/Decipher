About this policy

This policy describes how Decipher handles information relating to this website and project enquiries. Contact Deciphermediahouse@gmail.com with privacy questions.

Information you choose to share

The enquiry form asks for your name, email address, optional company name, selected service, and project brief. It prepares an email in your own email application. The website does not submit or store those form entries on a server. Decipher receives the information only when you send the email.

How information is used

Information you send is used to respond to your enquiry, discuss your requirements, prepare a proposal, and manage an agreed project. Enquiring does not subscribe you to promotional marketing.

Hosting and technical information

The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP address, browser information, and request time, to deliver and secure the website. The website does not add its own advertising trackers or analytics. Short preview clips are served with the website. A YouTube player is loaded only when you choose to watch a full film; YouTube may then process technical and playback information under its own privacy policy. Photographs and other page assets are served with the website.

Sharing and retention

Enquiry information may be processed by the email and service providers needed to communicate with you and deliver an agreed project. It is not sold. Information is retained only as needed for the enquiry, project, related business records, or applicable requirements. Contact us to ask about deletion or retention of your enquiry.

Your choices

You can choose not to send the enquiry, and may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you have shared. Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

Third-party services

Your email provider and YouTube process information according to their own policies. You can choose to watch the film directly on YouTube instead of loading the embedded player. Any additional tools, integrations, or data processing needed for a commissioned project will be discussed separately.

Updates

This policy may be updated as the website or services change. The date shown on this page identifies the latest revision.
